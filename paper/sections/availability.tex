The library (\texttt{tracetrim}, MIT licence), the experiment code, the frozen protocols with all deviations, the
aggregate results and the scripts that generate every number and table of this paper are released at
\RepositoryURL{} and archived on Zenodo with the dataset (DOI \SoftwareDOI). Traces of the literature-scan case (CC0 catalogue) and of the two
simulated cases are released in full. For the two cases built on the search-data API, whose terms do not license
redistribution of results, only derived per-call records are released: tool, field paths used, echoed and
judgeable, every field path seen, and token counts of the raw outputs. They are enough to recompute which fields
were used and the misses without the provider's data (\texttt{experiments/misses\_from\_derived.py} does, and checks
the result against Table~\ref{tab:phasea}), but not the token savings of those two cases, which need the payloads. The simulated servers
and the payload generator are included, with the exact versions of the OpenAPI descriptions they follow. For
Study 3 the repository adds the two frozen protocols with their deviations (\texttt{protocol-v04.md},
\texttt{protocol-v04-e3.md}), the aggregate results (\texttt{results/v04}, \texttt{results/v06}) and a summary of the
measures (\texttt{docs/measures-v04.md}); the traces of the two cases built on the search-data API, and the recorded
outputs of E3, are not redistributable either. Table~\ref{tab:availability} summarises what an independent reader can check, and with what.
