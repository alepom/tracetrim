The library (\texttt{tracetrim}, MIT licence), the experiment code, the frozen protocols with all deviations, the
aggregate results and the scripts that generate every number and table of this paper are released in the
repository that accompanies this preprint. Traces of the literature-scan case (CC0 catalogue) and of the simulated
cases are released in full; traces of the cases built on the search-data API are released only pseudonymised, as
its terms do not license redistribution of raw results. The simulated servers and the payload generator are
included, with the exact versions of the OpenAPI descriptions they follow.
