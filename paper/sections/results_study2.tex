\section{Study 2: safeguards}
\label{sec:study2}

Study~2 replayed the Phase B conversations again, \StudyTwoRunsPerArm{} runs per arm, with the projection learned
with both safeguards (arm \texttt{v02}) and with the same projection followed by structural compression
(Table~\ref{tab:study2}). It is a pre-registered check that the safeguards repair the specific failures that
motivated them, on the conversations already observed, not an independent validation on new tasks, runs or seeds.

\paragraph{Pre-registered hypotheses.} All four held as frozen. \emph{H5}: in billing support the \texttt{v02} arm
passed the frozen keyword test on all five tickets in \StudyTwoFrozenAllRightVtwo{} runs, the period end included,
where the 0.1 projection had lost it. The keyword tests accept a negated or contradictory sentence. Stricter
rules, added afterwards (deviation 15, Table~\ref{tab:c4audit}), read only the diagnosis and require the scenario's
exact amounts, the year and the outcome, rejecting negations: \AuditCFourDVtwo{} \texttt{v02} diagnoses pass them
against \AuditCFourDRaw{} raw ones, every \texttt{v02} failure being the decline reason discussed below. These are
still rules, not a reading of the answers; the difference is not a statistical result (five tickets per run are not
independent observations), but it shows that the hypotheses held as frozen do not by themselves mean that quality was
preserved. \emph{H6}: in the literature scan every \texttt{v02} run passed the gates
(\StudyTwoVtwoGatesiii), so no identifier was invented; with 0.1 the prudent arm had passed in
\PhaseBPrudentGatesiii{} runs. \emph{H7}: in CI triage every \texttt{v02} run found all four expected links,
including the one lost in Phase B. \emph{H8} is a token count on fixed replays, so it is
deterministic and equals the offline estimate given in the protocol: the \texttt{v02} arm still removed \StudyTwoVtwoTokensi,
\StudyTwoVtwoTokensiii, \StudyTwoVtwoTokensiv{} and \StudyTwoVtwoTokensv{} of the tool-message tokens in the
editorial plan, the literature scan, billing support and CI triage, against \StudyTwoHeadroomTokensi, \StudyTwoHeadroomTokensiii, \StudyTwoHeadroomTokensiv{} and
\StudyTwoHeadroomTokensv{} for Headroom (tokens only, in the configuration chosen; an illustration, not a comparison of methods) and
\ReserialMinReplayi, \ReserialMinReplayiii, \ReserialMinReplayiv{} and
\ReserialMinReplayv{} for a full minifier that removes no field (Figure~\ref{fig:savings}). Measured against JSON
already minified, the \texttt{v02} projection still removes \VtwoVsMini, \VtwoVsMiniii, \VtwoVsMiniv{} and
\VtwoVsMinv{} of the tokens in these four cases (Table~\ref{tab:savingsdetail}). The safeguards cost part of the saving (compare
\PhaseBPrudentTokensiii{} with \StudyTwoVtwoTokensiii{} in the literature scan) and bought back the three lost
pieces of information.

\paragraph{Outside the hypotheses.} In the editorial plan the \texttt{v02} arm \StudyTwoVtwoHoldsi{} under the band rule
(Table~\ref{tab:study2}): its gates passed in every run, but the median search volume of the keywords it chose was
\StudyTwoVtwoMedianVolumei, outside the raw arm's range (\StudyTwoRawBandVolumei), and on the computable part of the
protocol's list so was the mean (\StudyTwoVtwoMeanVolumei{} against \StudyTwoRawBandMeanVolumei). We read no direction into it: the
same projection followed by structural compression stayed in range (\StudyTwoVtwostructuralMedianVolumei), and in
Phase B the prudent arm was in range (\PhaseBPrudentMedianVolumei) while the aggressive and structural arms were not
(\PhaseBAggressiveMedianVolumei{} and \PhaseBStructuralMedianVolumei). With five runs per arm, search-volume measures
outside the raw range are a change to watch, not an established effect.

\paragraph{A fourth mechanism, found after the fact.} Reading the billing-support answers, we found that the
frozen test for one ticket was too lenient: it accepted the diagnosis when ``fondi'' (funds) or the stem
``insufficien'' appeared anywhere in the ticket, so advice such as ``check that you have sufficient funds'' passed it even when the diagnosis did not
give the reason for the declined payment. With a strict test on the diagnosis alone (added after the data were
seen, and reported beside the frozen result rather than instead of it), the raw arm gave the reason in
\StudyTwoStrictAllRightRaw{} runs and the \texttt{v02} arm in \StudyTwoStrictAllRightVtwo. The reason arrives as an
English sentence and the agent writes it in Italian: a \emph{translated} value, which neither usage detection nor
safeguard S2 recognises. The \texttt{v02} answers were not wrong (``the card was declined'') but less informative.

\paragraph{Cleaning and format change behaviour.} The structural arm adds two things to the \texttt{v02} projection:
cleaning, which removes nulls and empty values (inside lists too, which shifts the positions of the remaining
elements), and the TOON-style encoding. Together they changed answers. In the editorial plan the gates passed in \StudyTwoVtwostructuralGatesi{}
runs instead of \StudyTwoVtwoGatesi, each time on the same keyword: the search-volume tool, which the prompt names as
the source of all volumes, returned that keyword without a volume, while another tool gave one. With JSON the agent
used the other tool's figure, against the prompt's instruction to take volumes from the search-volume tool (the gate
checks the number, not its source); with the cleaned and encoded outputs it wrote zero. In billing support the arm had all tickets
right, under the frozen test, in \StudyTwoFrozenAllRightVtwostructural{} runs. The two changes cannot be told apart here (an arm
for each would be needed): what the data show is that the combined treatment shifted how the model resolved an
ambiguity. The evidence is thin (one keyword, five runs), and the encoder is the
library's own minimal TOON-style implementation, not the reference one \citep{schopplich2026toon}; but it is a
reminder that a format the model reads less often is not a neutral choice, and its saving has to be validated like
any other change to what the agent reads.

\paragraph{The content-gap case.} The content-gap analyst failed the gates in most runs of every arm, the raw one
included (\StudyTwoRawGatesii): when the search API returns a redirect link instead of a result's URL, the agent
reconstructs the URL from the breadcrumb, against the instructions. The safeguards neither caused nor fixed this,
and the projection saves little on this task (\StudyTwoVtwoTokensii): less than a full minifier
(\ReserialMinReplayii), which keeps every field. Where an agent cites most of what it receives, minifying is the
better choice.
