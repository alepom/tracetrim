# Protocol v3b · Does the learned projection preserve answer quality on hard billing tasks?

**Preregistered before calibration.** This file, the task generator, the key and the objective scorer are
committed before the first calibration run. The selected tasks, the schedule and the private mapping are frozen in
`protocol-v3b.lock` after calibration and before the campaign. Protocol v3 (`protocol-v3.md`) is unchanged:
v3b is a separate study with its own files (`experiments/v3b/`, `out/v3b/`).

## Question

Protocol v3 found that the prudent arm P preserves quality relative to raw outputs R, at a ceiling: 96.9% of
ratings were the maximum. v3b asks the same question on billing tasks where the facts the answer needs sit in
fields that P removes from list calls, and where the raw arm does not reach the maximum.

## Tasks, built hard

Case C4 only (billing, simulated Stripe-shaped server, `experiments/simulated/server_c4.py` unchanged, served
per task by `experiments/v3b/server_c4b.py`). Eighteen candidate tasks are generated by
`python -m experiments.v3b.worlds` with seed 130421. Each is a queue of five tickets with five new customers,
each ticket drawn from seven templates:

| Template | Trap or hidden fact | Hidden field in a list call (P) |
|---|---|---|
| `credit_note` | credit note issued after payment on the latest invoice | `stripe_list_invoices` `data[].post_payment_credit_notes_amount` |
| `balance` | current credit balance after a goodwill credit; the latest invoice shows an older balance | `stripe_search_customers` `data[].balance`, `data[].description` |
| `partial_refund` | partial refund of extra licences: how many, net paid | `stripe_list_charges` `data[].refunds.data[].description` |
| `replacement` | two void invoices, two replacements; the newest replacement is not the one asked about | `stripe_list_invoices` `data[].from_invoice.invoice` |
| `memo` | migration surcharge: the TB count is in the invoice memo | `stripe_list_invoices` `data[].description` |
| `failed_retry` | a declined charge followed by a successful one | none (trap) |
| `duplicate` | a duplicate account with a similar name under the same email | none (trap) |

All hidden values remain available to P through `expand`, and the detail endpoints (`stripe_retrieve_invoice`,
`stripe_retrieve_charge`, `stripe_list_refunds`) are not trimmed by the frozen policy. Amounts are never whole
euros.

**Hard by construction, checked.** `python -m experiments.v3b.hidden` sends each task's typical requests
(search each email, then list invoices, charges and subscriptions of every customer found) through the frozen P
engine of protocol v3 (`experiments/v3/material/policies/C4/P`, same hashes, no relearning). A hidden fact is
verified when its value is at its path in the original result and the path is absent from what P delivers.
Every candidate has at least one verified hidden fact (79 of 79 verified, 3 to 6 per task).

**Novelty.** `python -m experiments.v3b.novelty`: no identifier or email of the v3b worlds appears in the v3
material, the v3 traces, the original study's C4 traces or the C4 prompts, and no ticket question repeats a v3
or historical one.

**The key** is written in each task file before any run: a list of facts per ticket (customer, identifiers,
amounts, counts). The judge never sees it.

## Arms, model, runs

- **R**: unaltered output of the simulated tools.
- **P**: the frozen prudent policy of protocol v3 (default identifier protection, compact JSON, recoverable
  hidden fields), `expand` and the base recovery instructions (`tracetrim.skill.text(done=False)`).

Same model, settings, loop, guards, retry rule, ledger rules and trace format as protocol v3
(`experiments/v3/live.py`): `gpt-5.6-terra`, Responses API, default inference settings, 40 turns, 80 calls,
one retry of an infrastructure failure. A fresh engine and conversation per run. System prompt
`experiments/prompts/c4-system.md`, user prompt the task's literal prompt.

## Calibration (before the freeze)

Run **R once per candidate** (18 runs, private directory `out/v3b/calibration/`). Score each with the objective
scorer (below). No judge.

**Selection rule, fixed now:**
1. Eligible: candidates with R's fact share **s between 0.40 and 0.90 inclusive**.
2. If 12 or more are eligible, keep the 12 with s closest to 0.65; ties by task number, ascending.
3. If 10 or 11 are eligible, keep them all.
4. If fewer than 10 are eligible, stop and report: the tasks are revised.

Calibration runs never enter the campaign or its analysis. The tasks are selected on R's performance in an
independent run. Both arms then run afresh on the same tasks, so the selection biases no arm by design. Any
residual regression toward R's typical level works against P, by the reading fixed before this study.

**Budget rule.** Before the campaign, estimate the cost of the remaining campaign and judging. Use the mean
cost per calibration run, P costed like R, and the measured v3 judge cost per call scaled by the task's input
tokens. Add the spend of protocol v3 and of the calibration, all on the same private v3 ledgers and caps. If
the estimate exceeds the warning, first reduce to 10 tasks, dropping those farthest from 0.65, then to 2
repetitions. If it still exceeds the warning, stop and ask. Figures stay private.

## Campaign

Selected tasks × **3 repetitions** × 2 arms (unless reduced by the budget rule), in blocks (task, repetition)
shuffled with seed 130422. Within a block the arm order alternates R–P and P–R, assigned by shuffling a
balanced list with the same seed. Every block serves both arms of one task. The schedule is written to
`experiments/v3b/material/schedule.json` at the freeze. Caps: the private v3 warning and stop, cumulative over
protocol v3, calibration, campaign and judging. Pause at the warning for review, refuse new calls at the stop.

## Outcomes and analysis, fixed now

**Primary: judged composite (0–4).** Same judge as the v3 deviations, part of this protocol from the start:
- `gemini-3.8-flash` on Vertex AI, global endpoint, thinking `HIGH`, structured output;
- full v3 rubric (`experiments/v3/rubric.md`), cards grouped by task, the reference evidence once;
- the task's six answers (2 arms × 3 repetitions) under neutral labels in random order, each scored on its own;
- order seeds 130431, 130432, 130433 for passes 1, 2, 3;
- two independent passes, then a third reading of any dimension differing by two points or more;
- the coordinator's rules for combining scores (`experiments/v3/coordinator.py`), mapping sealed privately with
  only its hash in the lock, arm cues scrubbed, malformed outputs asked once more and then reported.

Composite = mean of the three dimensions; per dimension, the mean of the two passes or the third reading where
adjudicated.

**H1b.** P − R ≥ −0.5 on the composite. P minus R within repetition, averaged within task, tasks averaged
equally. Percentile bootstrap, 95%, 10,000 draws, seed 311, resampling tasks with all their repetitions and
both arms. Preservation requires the lower endpoint strictly above −0.5. An interval spanning −0.5 is
inconclusive, and one wholly below it is a material loss. Report the point estimate and the full interval. At
−0.25: descriptive only.

**Co-primary, objective: share of key facts reported correctly**, by `experiments/v3b/facts.py`, no judge:
- each key ticket matched by id, each fact looked up in that ticket object;
- customer id equal, identifier present, amount in euros with two decimals, count followed by its unit;
- missing or unparsable answers score zero.

P − R with the same estimator and interval. No equivalence margin is claimed. An interval wholly below zero
is reported as a loss of facts.

**Secondary:**
- gates (`experiments.checks.check`, as in v3) and grounding (`tracetrim.grounding.unsupported`);
- share of hidden facts reported correctly, by arm;
- in P, `expand` calls and runs, and for each hidden fact whether its value reached the model, through a
  trimmed list (no, by construction), `expand`, or an untrimmed detail call;
- calls, turns, tokens and cost relative to R;
- agreement between the passes (exact, within one point, quadratic-weighted kappa) and third readings.

**Missing data:** as protocol v3. Main contrasts on complete pairs; 0/4 worst and best bounds on the
composite and 0/1 bounds on the fact share for infrastructure losses.

## Limits stated in advance

- One agent model, one synthetic task family, 10–12 tasks.
- Tasks selected on R's calibration performance.
- An AI judge that knows the design but not the arm.
- Grouped judging (possible contrast effects, common to both arms).
- The objective scorer checks the presence of facts, not the reasoning around them.
- The hidden-fact check uses typical requests. A live agent may call detail endpoints that P does not trim.
