# Protocol v3c · Does the skill's absence check recover the facts the projection hides?

**Preregistered on 21 November 2026, before any run.** The selected tasks, the schedule, the private mapping and
the library under test are frozen in `protocol-v3c.lock` before the first paid run. Protocols v3 and v3b are
unchanged; v3c has its own files (`experiments/v3c/`, `out/v3c/`) and its own private spend caps (`v3c`).

## Question

In protocol v3b the prudent arm P, on billing tasks that need fields the frozen projection removes, reported
83.75% of those facts against 100% with raw outputs. The loss came from free-text fields: the agent did not
recover a customer's `description` or an invoice memo, and stated that the information did not exist. Version
2 of the tracetrim skill (library commit `ab49a5a`, `tracetrim.skill.VERSION = "2"`) adds one rule to the skill
and to the description of `expand`: before saying information is missing or unknown, check whether a hidden field
could hold it and expand it first.

**Does version 2 recover more of the hidden facts than version 1, and does it cost more where nothing is hidden?**
The answer decides whether version 2 is the default of tracetrim 1.4.

## Arms

| Arm | Projection | Skill and `expand` text |
|---|---|---|
| P1 | frozen prudent policy P of protocol v3 (`experiments/v3/material/policies/C4/P`), no relearning | version 1 (the text of 1.3, used in v3 and v3b) |
| P2 | the same policy | version 2 |

The two arms differ **only** in the text. Model, settings, loop, guards, retry rule and trace format are those
of protocols v3 and v3b (`experiments/v3/live.py`): `gpt-5.6-terra`, Responses API, default inference settings,
40 turns, 80 calls, and one retry of an infrastructure failure. A fresh engine and conversation per run.
Raw outputs (R) are not run again: R of protocol v3b is the historical reference for secondary contrasts only.

## Tasks and runs

- **Hard tasks.** The 12 tasks of protocol v3b, version 2 (`C4b-01` to `C4b-12`), with their worlds, keys
  and 54 hidden facts unchanged. Each task runs **3 repetitions × 2 arms = 72 runs**.
- **Ordinary tasks, a cost and quality check.** The 10 billing tasks of protocol v3 (`C4-01` to `C4-10`),
  where the facts sit in fields the projection keeps. Each task runs **1 repetition × 2 arms = 20 runs**.

The schedule is in `experiments/v3c/material/schedule.json`, generated by `experiments/v3c/run.py` with seed
130511: blocks (task, repetition) are shuffled, both arms run in each block, and the arm order comes from a
balanced list shuffled with the same seed. Every block serves both arms of one task. The 92 runs are new: no
earlier run is reused.

## Outcomes and analysis, fixed now

**Primary: hidden-fact share on the hard tasks.** For each answer, the share of the key's hidden facts reported
correctly, by `experiments/v3b/facts.py` (whole cents accepted, as in v3b version 2).
- **Contrast:** P2 minus P1, paired within repetition, averaged within task, tasks averaged equally.
- **Interval:** 95% percentile bootstrap, 10,000 draws, seed 311, resampling tasks with all their repetitions
  and both arms.
- **H1c:** version 2 recovers more, if the lower end of the interval is above 0. An interval containing 0 is no
  improvement shown, and one wholly below 0 is a worsening.

**Secondary outcomes:**
- **On the hard tasks:**
  - the share of all key facts (P2 − P1);
  - the two free-text facts of v3b (the origin of the last credit, the TB of the memo) by arm;
  - `expand` calls and runs;
  - for each hidden fact, whether its value reached the model (`expand`, an untrimmed detail call, or not at
    all; the v3b check, with its known error on the replacement link reported as such);
  - cost per run P2/P1;
  - P2 − R and P1 − R on the hidden-fact share, against R of protocol v3b. This is historical, not concurrent,
    and descriptive only.
- **On the ordinary tasks:**
  - cost per run P2/P1 (sum over the 10 tasks);
  - calls, turns and `expand` calls;
  - gates (`experiments.checks.check`);
  - grounding (`tracetrim.grounding.unsupported`).
- **Judged composite (Gemini), hard tasks only,** if the spend rule below allows it: P2 − P1 with the same
  estimator, descriptive. The judge is that of v3b: `gemini-3.8-flash`, Vertex AI global endpoint, thinking
  `HIGH`, the full v3 rubric, one call per task with the evidence once. Each call carries the task's 6 answers
  under neutral labels in a seeded order (seeds 130531, 130532, 130533), and each answer is scored on its own.
  There are two passes, plus a third reading for any dimension differing by two points or more. The mapping is
  sealed privately and arm cues are scrubbed.

**Decision rule for the default of 1.4, fixed now.** Version 2 becomes the default only if all three hold:
1. H1c holds: the lower end of the interval is above 0.
2. On the ordinary tasks P2 does not cost more than 10% above P1 (sum of LLM API costs at list prices, ratio
   ≤ 1.10).
3. On the ordinary tasks P2 passes at least as many gates as P1.

Otherwise version 1 stays the default and version 2 is an option (`--skill-version 2`); the result is reported
either way.

**Missing data.** As in protocol v3: complete pairs, with 0/1 worst and best bounds on the shares for
infrastructure losses.

## Spend

The private caps are in the `v3c` entry of `experiments/caps.local.json`, separate from `v3`. Every provider
attempt is accounted for. The campaign pauses at the warning for review and refuses new calls at the stop. Figures
stay private.

**Spend rule for the judge**, after the campaign:
- if the spend so far plus the judge's upper bound (two passes and a third reading of every task, at the
  measured v3b cost per call) exceeds the warning, the judge runs two passes without third readings;
- if even two passes exceed it, the judge is not run, and the primary and the other secondary outcomes stand.

## Limits stated in advance

- One agent model, one synthetic task family.
- The hard tasks were built to need removed fields, and were the ones on which the text was designed. A text
  that helps here may help less on other tasks, which the ordinary-task check only partly covers.
- R is historical.
- An AI judge.
- The objective scorer checks the presence of facts, not the reasoning around them.
